# Hospital Antimicrobial Stewardship Policy

## 1. Restricted Antimicrobials (Requires ID Specialist Pre-authorization)
The following reserve antibiotics cannot be dispensed without written approval from an Infectious Disease Specialist or Clinical Pharmacist within 24 hours of initiation:
- **Meropenem / Imipenem** (Carbapenems)
- **Colistin / Polymyxin B**
- **Vancomycin (IV)** (Requires serum trough monitoring before 4th dose)
- **Linezolid (IV and Oral)**
- **Tigecycline**
- **Caspofungin / Voriconazole / Amphotericin B**

## 2. Surgical Prophylaxis Protocols
- **Timing**: Single dose administered within 60 minutes prior to surgical incision (120 minutes for Vancomycin or Fluoroquinolones).
- **Standard agent**: Cefazolin 2g IV (3g if patient weight > 120 kg).
- **Penicillin/Cephalosporin Allergy**: Clindamycin 900mg IV or Vancomycin 15mg/kg IV.
- **Duration**: Prophylaxis must NOT extend beyond 24 hours post-operatively for uncomplicated surgical procedures.

## 3. Vancomycin Therapeutic Drug Monitoring (TDM)
- Target trough concentration: **15 - 20 mcg/mL** for severe infections (MRSA bacteremia, endocarditis, osteomyelitis, hospital-acquired pneumonia).
- Target trough concentration: **10 - 15 mcg/mL** for non-severe skin and soft tissue infections.
- Trough blood draw must occur 30 minutes before the 4th scheduled dose.
