# Microbiology Cultures, Antibiotic Susceptibility Testing (AST) & Infection Control Guidelines

## 1. Overview
The Microbiology module in HIMES manages bacterial, fungal, and mycobacterial cultures, direct microscopy, organism identification, automated susceptibility testing, and institutional infection control alerts following CLSI (M100) and EUCAST clinical breakpoint guidelines.

## 2. Direct Microscopy & Urgent Smear Reporting
- **Gram Stain**: Direct visualization within 1-2 hours of specimen accessioning.
  * *Gram-Positive Cocci in clusters*: Suspect *Staphylococcus aureus* / CoNS.
  * *Gram-Positive Cocci in pairs/chains*: Suspect *Streptococcus pneumoniae*, *Enterococcus spp.*, or Group A/B Strep.
  * *Gram-Negative Bacilli*: Suspect Enterobacteriaceae (*E. coli*, *Klebsiella*, *Enterobacter*) or Non-fermenters (*Pseudomonas aeruginosa*, *Acinetobacter*).
  * *Gram-Negative Diplococci*: Intracellular: Suspect *Neisseria meningitidis* (CSF) or *Neisseria gonorrhoeae* (Urogenital).
- **Acid-Fast Bacilli (AFB / Ziehl-Neelsen / Auramine-O Fluorescent)**: Screening for *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*. Immediate public health and airborne isolation notification if positive.
- **KOH Wet Mount**: Detection of fungal hyphae, pseudohyphae, and budding yeast cells.
- **India Ink Preparation**: Rapid detection of encapsulated *Cryptococcus neoformans* in CSF.

## 3. Specimen Processing & Culture Inoculation Protocols
- **Blood Cultures**: Inoculate automated continuous-monitoring system (e.g., BD BACTEC / BacT/ALERT). Standard incubation: 5 days (extended to 14-21 days for suspected *Brucella* or infective endocarditis).
  * *Positive Bottle Signal*: Immediate Gram stain and verbal notification to attending clinician within **30 minutes**.
- **Urine Culture**: Inoculate CLED / MacConkey and 5% Sheep Blood agar using a calibrated 1 µL loop.
  * Significant bacteriuria threshold: $\ge 10^5 \text{ CFU/mL}$ of a single uropathogen ($\ge 10^3 \text{ CFU/mL}$ in catheterized or symptomatic pregnant patients).
  * $> 2$ bacterial species without a dominant pathogen indicates probable perineal flora contamination; request repeat clean-catch specimen.
- **Sputum & Respiratory**: Evaluate Bartlett's Score / Murray-Washington criteria under low-power (100x) field:
  * Acceptable for culture: $> 25$ Polymorphonuclear leukocytes (PMNs) and $< 10$ Squamous Epithelial Cells (SECs) per low-power field.
  * Salivary contamination ($> 10$ SECs): Reject and request fresh early morning deep expectoration.

## 4. Antibiotic Susceptibility Testing (AST) Interpretation & Cascade Reporting
HIMES supports both Kirby-Bauer disk diffusion zone measurements (mm) and automated Minimum Inhibitory Concentration (MIC, µg/mL).

### Susceptibility Categories (CLSI / EUCAST):
- **Susceptible (S)**: High likelihood of therapeutic success with standard dosing regimen.
- **Susceptible, Increased Exposure / Intermediate (I)**: Therapeutic success achievable with adjusted higher dosing, increased frequency, or site-specific physiological concentration (e.g., urinary concentration).
- **Resistant (R)**: High likelihood of therapeutic failure; antimicrobial agent should not be selected.

### Selective / Cascade Antimicrobial Reporting Policy:
To prevent indiscriminate use of broad-spectrum reserve agents and support Antimicrobial Stewardship:
- **Group A (Primary / First-Line Panel)**: Routinely tested and reported for all isolates (e.g., Ampicillin, Cefazolin, Ciprofloxacin, Gentamicin for Enterobacteriaceae; Penicillin, Oxacillin/Cefoxitin, Erythromycin, Clindamycin for *S. aureus*).
- **Group B (Secondary / Broad-Spectrum Panel)**: Tested routinely but reported **only if** the organism is resistant to Group A agents, if the patient has documented severe allergy/contraindication, or if isolated from a critical sterile body site (e.g., Cefepime, Piperacillin-Tazobactam, Meropenem, Amikacin, Vancomycin).
- **Group C / Reserve Panel (Restricted)**: Reported only after approval from Infectious Disease / Antimicrobial Stewardship Committee (e.g., Colistin, Linezolid, Tigecycline, Ceftazidime-Avibactam).

## 5. Multidrug-Resistant Organism (MDRO) Critical Alert Protocol
Detection of any of the following triggers an automated electronic Infection Prevention & Control (IPC) alert and immediate contact isolation tag in HIMES:
1. **MRSA (Methicillin-Resistant *Staphylococcus aureus*)**: Oxacillin MIC $\ge 4\ \mu\text{g/mL}$ or positive Cefoxitin disk screen ($\text{zone} \le 21\text{ mm}$ or $mecA$ gene positive).
2. **VRE (Vancomycin-Resistant *Enterococcus faecalis / faecium*)**: Vancomycin MIC $\ge 32\ \mu\text{g/mL}$ ($vanA / vanB$ phenotype).
3. **ESBL (Extended-Spectrum Beta-Lactamase Producers)**: Resistance to 3rd generation cephalosporins (Ceftriaxone, Ceftazidime, Cefotaxime) confirmed by Clavulanate synergy.
4. **CRE / CPO (Carbapenem-Resistant Enterobacteriaceae / Carbapenemase-Producing Organisms)**: Resistance to Meropenem, Imipenem, or Ertapenem (NDM, KPC, OXA-48, VIM, IMP).
5. **CRAB (Carbapenem-Resistant *Acinetobacter baumannii*)** and **CRPA (Carbapenem-Resistant *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*)**.
6. **Pan-Drug Resistant (PDR) / Extensively Drug-Resistant (XDR) Isolates**.
