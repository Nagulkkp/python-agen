# Patient Billing and Cashless Insurance Policy

## 1. Cashless Insurance Pre-Authorization Process
- Planned Admissions: Pre-authorization request must be submitted to the Third-Party Administrator (TPA) / Insurer at least 48 hours prior to scheduled admission.
- Emergency Admissions: Initial cashless intimation and emergency claim request must be submitted within 6 hours of patient admission.
- Required documents: Government ID, Health Insurance Card, Doctor's Admission Note with provisional diagnosis, and estimated cost certificate.

## 2. Discharge Billing Clearance Workflow
1. **Medical Discharge Summary Generated**: Attending physician signs off discharge order and completed discharge summary by 10:00 AM.
2. **Pharmacy Return & Settlement**: Any unused unsealed medications returned to inpatient pharmacy within 1 hour.
3. **Final Bill Compilation**: Central billing generates final itemized bill with all procedure, lab, pharmacy, and bed charges.
4. **Final TPA Approval**: For insured patients, final bill and discharge summary are uploaded to insurance portal. Expected turnaround: 2 to 3 hours.
5. **Gate Pass Issuance**: Once cashless approval is received or co-pay / non-medical expenses are settled, billing desk issues the electronic Gate Pass.
